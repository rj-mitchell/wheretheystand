Devolved parliaments

The Scottish Parliament, the Senedd and the Northern Ireland Assembly decide health, education, housing and much else for their nations. Read and search what their members said.

From each parliament's official report, via TheyWorkForYou: the Northern Ireland Assembly from 1998, the Scottish Parliament from 1999 and the Senedd from 2016 (TheyWorkForYou's start), older years added a few dozen sittings at a time. Senedd contributions are shown in English; those made in Welsh are the official translation. Briefings on the main page cover Westminster unless you tick “Devolved parliaments” under Settings; they then report these parliaments separately.

Elsewhere: each parliament's own record, with committees and earlier sessions: the Scottish Parliament Official Report, the Senedd Record of Proceedings and the Northern Ireland Assembly Official Report; TheyWorkForYou covers all three from their early sessions.
