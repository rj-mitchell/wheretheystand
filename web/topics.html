Topic briefings

Where MPs and peers stand on the issues Parliament keeps returning to, kept up to date as new debates, questions and statements arrive.

Each is written in the same way as any other briefing: from the official record only, with each point linked to its source. A topic is rewritten when Parliament has said something new on it, at most once a week, and earlier versions are kept. For anything not listed here, ask your own question. For impartial background and statistics on each subject, the House of Commons Library is the place to start.

    How topics are chosen

    Topics cover the subjects most debated in Parliament, across every area of policy, and each question is worded neutrally: it names no side and asks what positions have been taken, not whether any of them is right. To suggest a topic, email admin@wheretheystand.uk.
