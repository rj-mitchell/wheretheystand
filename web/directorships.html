Company roles of MPs and peers

Companies where members of the Commons and the Lords are directors, company secretaries or members of a partnership, from the Companies House register. Open any member's page for their earlier roles too.

Companies House does not link its records to Parliament. A record is used only when the surname, a first or middle name and the month and year of birth all match the member's, so a few may be missed (for example members whose birth month is not known) and, rarely, one may belong to someone else: every entry links to Companies House. See how they are matched. Holding a company role is not a finding of wrongdoing. What members must declare, including shareholdings, is in their register of interests, shown on their page.

Elsewhere: who owns and controls a company, and its accounts, are on Companies House.
