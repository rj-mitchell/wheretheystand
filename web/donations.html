Donations to MPs

Political donations to MPs, and to their local parties, as reported to the Electoral Commission. Search for a donor, or open any MP's page to see the donations they received.

The Electoral Commission publishes donations above the reporting limits, with the donor's name. A donation is not a finding of wrongdoing. Donations are matched to MPs by name and date, so a few may be missing; every entry links to the Commission's own record. See how they are matched. Donations to parties nationally, loans, and election spending are not shown here: search them on the Electoral Commission's register. Who owns and runs a company that gave is on Companies House.
