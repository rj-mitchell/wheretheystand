All-party groups

All-party parliamentary groups are informal groups of MPs and peers on a subject or a country. Many are run by outside organisations, and some are funded by companies, charities or campaign groups.

From the Register of All-Party Parliamentary Groups, via mySociety's APPG dataset (CC BY 4.0). Being an officer or member of a group is not an endorsement of its funders. See how this is put together. The official Register of All-Party Parliamentary Groups has each group's full entry and its income and expenditure statements.
