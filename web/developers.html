Data and API

Researchers, journalists and developers can use the data behind this site directly. Everything here is free, read-only and needs no key. None of it uses AI: briefings are only available on the site.

Terms and fair use

  • Parliamentary information is licensed under the Open Parliament Licence v3.0; please credit it, and Where MPs Stand, when you use it.
  • Requests are limited per visitor (about 20 a minute, shared with the rest of the site). If you get a 429 response, wait a minute. Please do not use the API to copy the whole archive: the sources listed below are better for that.
  • The API is in beta and may change.

Search the record

GET /api/v1/search?q=onshore wind returns the contributions in the Westminster record since 1997 that best match the words, ranked by relevance (by keyword and by meaning), with the speaker as they were on the day. It may take a few seconds.

q
The words to search for (required).
date_from, date_to
Limit to dates, written as YYYY-MM-DD.
house
commons or lords.
party
A party identifier, for example labour, conservative, liberal-democrat, scottish-national-party, reform, green.
written
false to leave out written questions, answers and statements.
limit
Up to 50 results (default 20).

Each result has the record's identifier (TheyWorkForYou's), date, House, chamber, type, heading, speaker (name, party, constituency and parlparse person identifier), an excerpt, the Hansard column or question number, links to the official record and TheyWorkForYou, and a relevance score between 0 and 1.

Other endpoints

These return JSON and are what the site's own pages use.

/api/people?q=, /api/people/{number}
MPs and peers: memberships, roles, recent contributions, voting record, registered interests, early day motions.
/api/debates/{date}/{chamber}/{slug}
A whole debate in order. chamber is commons, lords, westminster-hall or public-bill-committee; the address is the same as the debate's page on this site.
/api/week?start=YYYY-MM-DD
One sitting week: debates by day and chamber, every vote with its kind, written statements.
/api/bills, /api/bills/{slug}
Bills since 1997, their sittings and their votes, with what each vote was on.
/api/trends?q=&by=
How often words were used each year, with totals for context. by is all, party, house, nation or parliaments.
/api/edms?q=&person=, /api/edms/{id}
Early day motions and who signed them, with each member's party on the day they signed.
/api/committees, /api/committees/{id}, /api/inquiries/{id}, /api/witnesses?q=
Committees, their inquiries, reports and government responses, and who gave oral evidence.
/api/devolved, /api/devolved/search?q=&leg=, /api/devolved/{leg}/{key}
The Scottish Parliament (scottish-parliament), the Senedd (senedd) and the Northern Ireland Assembly (ni-assembly).

RSS feeds

  • /feeds/people/{number}.xml: what a member has said, newest first.
  • /feeds/topics.xml, /feeds/bills.xml, /feeds/edms.xml, /feeds/committee-reports.xml, /feeds/corrections.xml.
