The lighter side

Parliament is not all serious. Hansard notes when the House laughs, and members table motions about prize pies and pub cats. Here are some of those moments, from every side, as the record has them.

Found in the record by plain rules: a speech the Official Report marks “[Laughter.]”, or an early day motion whose title is about food, animals, birthdays or records. Tributes and grave subjects are left out. No AI chooses or describes them, and they come with no comment. Each links to where it was said, so it can be read in context.
