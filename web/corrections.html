Report a problem

If a briefing gets something wrong, misrepresents what someone said, or you have any other concern, please tell us. The quickest way is the "Report a problem" link under the briefing itself, which tells us which briefing you mean. You can also use this form, or email admin@wherempsstand.com.

Briefings are written by AI from the official record. Where a briefing is wrong, it is withdrawn and listed above. The record itself is Parliament's: to correct Hansard, contact the Hansard editors.
